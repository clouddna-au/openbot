### Fixed

- When you open the app again after more than 30 seconds away, it connects to the server about
  2.5 seconds sooner. Before, it first tried to keep the old connection, and the server always
  refused it.
- While the app connects to the server again, the open chat keeps its colors and its voice button.
  You can read, scroll and write as usual, and the line above the composer shows the attempts.
  Before, the chat turned grey after 5 seconds.
- A chat that you open loads before the chats that the app loads for later. Photos, tasks and
  routines load after those chats.
